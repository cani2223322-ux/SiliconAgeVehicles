# Engineering Workbench
A workbench (MV) with a 5 x 5 grid: it makes every component (3 x 3 recipes, instant) and every vehicle (5 x 5, for EU and time). Core upgrade slots: QV-SV vehicles need a transformer upgrade (HV-IV input). "Lay out" fills the grid from your inventory, the arrow lists the recipes; with NEI every workbench recipe shows on its "Engineering Workbench" page (R - how to make, U - where used).
@recipe craft:siliconagevehicles:workbench

# Repair garage
A controller and floor plates round it. Repairs the hull and cools the motor for EU and steel, shows a repair hologram, outputs a comparator signal. **Tuning** is fitted here too (up to 4 a vehicle): Motor+ I / II, Battery+, Armour, Light hull, Big wheels, Light bar, Reinforced drill, Extra tank, Radar-absorbing coating, Smooth hull, Radar warning receiver - all visible on the model. "Service battery" and "Repair all" (with hydraulic fluid for the liner) are here as well.
@recipe wb:garage
@recipe wb:garagePad

# Gas Column
The column (HV) holds tanks of every core gas and refuels its owner's vehicles within 4 blocks: hydrogen - fuel, helium - cooling, argon - the drill's hull field, oxygen - the shuttle's cabin, deuterium - the long-range mode.
@recipe wb:gasColumn

# Roads
Asphalt gives +30 % speed and half the wear, concrete +20 %. Markings (edge, dashed, solid, arrows), curb, signs, a redstone traffic light, a guard rail. All on a plain crafting table from vanilla items.

# Trailers and the key card
**Crawler trailers**: cargo (+54 slots), tanker (16,000 mB of a fluid or gas, refuels vehicles nearby), platform (carries a buggy or a hoverbike), mobile charger (4M EU, solar).
**Key card**: Drive / Passenger / Cargo / Repair rights per player, modes Open / Friends / Owner, change the lock. A blank card: right-click a vehicle - the owner's card, Shift + right-click - the access screen.
@recipe wb:keyCard
**Vehicle map** (M while riding or the station's button): where your vehicles are, their charge and wear.
