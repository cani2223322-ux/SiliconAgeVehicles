# The vehicles at a glance
Eleven vehicles, each with its own niche. The numbers on their pages come from the game itself. Every vehicle is assembled on the Engineering Workbench (a 5 x 5 grid, for EU and time); QV-SV vehicles need the core's transformer upgrades in the workbench's slots.
- No vehicle goes faster than 180 km/h (the server keeps loading the world ahead).
- Aircraft fly up to Y 200; without power a helicopter autorotates, a plane glides 1:8; seated riders take no fall damage.
- Hull wear, motor heat with a stall (helium cools), limp mode without charge, owner and access, a per-player limit.
- Foreign private fields are never touched: the drill, jumps and arrivals check the protection.

# Hoverbike
Hovers over ground, water and lava: the cushion runs on the battery, the drive on hydrogen. Space - higher; F - "Boost" (x1.35). Helium cools the motor.

@model HOVER
@recipe wb:hover

# Light Helicopter
Vertical take-off and hover. Space - collective up, X / Ctrl - down, W / S - forward and back, A / D - turn. Without power - autorotation. The C+ cockpit "Hybrid + overhead panel": a lamp strip, a radar altimeter with callouts, a hover indicator, a floor window.

@model HELI
@recipe wb:heli

# Light Plane
Cheap long trips. A take-off run (half as long on "Runway" blocks), flaps [ and ], airbrake U, the autopilot (panel - I) and a flight plan on the map. The C+ cockpit "Hybrid + MFD".

# Cargo Crawler
Tracks: turns on the spot, 2-block steps, swims. Hitches a trailer (cargo, tanker, platform, mobile charger). Auto-delivery: right-click the crawler with a core Coordinator to set points A and B - it drives between them by itself.

# Drilling Vehicle
3 x 3 or 5 x 5 tunnels (with the drill widener), "ore only" and levelling modes. B - drill on / off, N - mode. Argon feeds the hull field (-40 % damage), helium cools. The ore goes into its big hold.

# Transport Shuttle
Free flight in every direction on EU (200 EU/t). A sealed cabin: the crew breathes oxygen under water (1 mB/s a person). Above Y 120 at speed N - the long-range mode on deuterium (1 mB/s instead of EU). Fly into an open vortex of the core's Ground or Space Bridge and the shuttle comes out on the other side with its people and cargo.

@model SHUTTLE
@recipe wb:shuttle

# Singular Ship
Everything the shuttle does, faster (400 EU/t).
- V - "Jump": a portal to the point of a core Coordinator in its hold or home (Y - pick the target). 16 blocks or more; 20M EU + 2M per 1000 blocks and 10 mB + 10 per 1000 blocks of singular matter (another dimension - 100M EU and 100 mB); a 30 s cooldown.
- Z - gravity anchor (40 EU/t), C - invisibility to hostile mobs (1 mB/s singular matter).
