# The config file
Everything is in config/SiliconAgeVehicles.cfg, categories vehicles, airdefense, airport and book. Almost everything new can be switched off; client options (look, sound) act only for you, server ones for everyone. The vehicles' function keys live apart - in config/SiliconAgeVehicles_keys.cfg (set them in the vehicle menu).

# vehicles: the vehicles
@kv wear | hull wear
@kv maxPerPlayer | vehicles per player (0 - no limit)
@kv fuelMode | HYDROGEN (default) or EU
@kv batteryWear | battery wear
@kv tuning, locks | tuning, key cards and access
@kv stationBoost | the charging station's boost
@kv stationsAddUp | stations on one pad add up
@kv singularJump, mobCloak | the ship's jump and invisibility
@kv regen | regenerative braking
@kv cruise, autopilot | cruise and autopilot
@kv ins, insAlignSeconds | INS and its alignment time (10 s)
@kv engineStart, failures | engine start and failures
@kv zoneFrames | air-defence zone frames on the HUD
## Look and sound (client)
@kv textures, liveries | textures and liveries
@kv headlights, dynamicLight | headlight beams and block light
@kv navLights, animations | nav lights, animations
@kv glassCockpit, cockpitHiRes | cockpit displays, sharp font
@kv cockpitQuality | AUTO / 1K / 2K / 4K
@kv sounds, vehicleCamera | sounds, the vehicle camera
@kv icons3d | 3D models of vehicle items

# airdefense: air defence
@kv enabled, mode | air defence on, warnings (default) or fire
@kv minWarningSeconds | the wait after the warning
@kv fireOnFriends, requirePvp | fire at friends (not in the CODE-ONLY mode), only with PvP
@kv maxRadarsPerPlayer, maxLaunchersPerPlayer | limits
@kv minDistanceFromSpawn | not nearer the spawn
@kv upgrades, keepModules | core modules, modules kept in the item
@kv rcsExtras, coverageMap | detectability, the coverage map
@kv iff, iffHandoff, iffResponseSeconds | the tower's IFF and the handoff
@kv autoSquawk, airportFriends | airport codes
@kv allTransponders | a transponder on every vehicle, the ground ones too (on)
@kv keyClock | the day key's clock: total (the world's total time) or day
@kv codeOnly, codeOnlyManual | the CODE-ONLY mode allowed, a code dialled by hand (both on)
@kv codeOnlyArmSeconds, codeOnlyConfirmSeconds | CODE-ONLY: arming (60 s), between two misses (5 s)
@kv codeOnlyMinWarningSeconds | CODE-ONLY: the wait to fire for everybody (20 s)
@kv codeOnlyFire | CODE-ONLY: fire at a board without the right code with mode = warnings too (on; false - as mode)
@kv codeOnlyEmergencySeconds | CODE-ONLY: 7700's grace per aircraft (180 s; 0 - never a target)
@kv codeOnlyOverlapSeconds, codeCardMaxPeriods | the last code after a period change (60 s), the longest card term (7)
@kv codeOnlyProbeCodes, codeOnlyProbeMinutes | guessing: wrong codes within 2 min (4), the GUESSING block (5 min)
@kv fireInProtectedRegions | fire at targets in others' protected fields
@kv damageMultiplier | air-defence damage x (none with vehicles.wear = false)
@kv noRadioDecisionSeconds, noRadioMemoryMinutes | 7600: the post's time to decide, the episodes' memory
@kv terrainCache, terrainCacheRadius | the terrain memory of unloaded chunks
@kv zoneMaxDistance, maxZonesPerPlayer | a zone from its post, zones a player
@kv embassyAd | the embassy's air defence (on; off - the emplacements are decor)
@kv embassyWarnPerDay, embassyWarnSeconds | embassy AD: warnings a game day (1; 0 - fire at once), seconds to turn away (10)
@kv embassyCloseBlocks, embassyMinHeight | embassy AD: fire at once when 32 closer or below 40 over the field during the turn
@kv embassyUnmanned | embassy AD: a machine in the air with nobody aboard is a target after its owner's warning (on)
@kv models | 3D models of the structures (client)
