# Vehicles
## The vehicle won't start
Check the hydrogen (the H2 bar on the HUD), the battery (the slot on the cargo screen's "Vehicle" tab, "NO BATTERY" / "BATTERY FLAT") and the battery's health: under 50 % the engine starts on the 2nd or 3rd try, under 20 % not at all. Jump-start it from another vehicle.
## The motor overheated and stopped
Wait for it to cool or refuel helium - it cools. In the garage the motor cools at once.
## It won't pick up
Only the owner (or a player with the repair right), the vehicle empty and standing. The liner - on the ground, engines off. A core wrench picks it up at once.
## The vehicle snaps back at speed
The server can't keep up: load the chunks ahead or go slower. The [SVA-MOVE] lines in latest.log help find the cause - attach them to a report.
## A key does nothing
The function keys are set in the vehicle menu (`) and work only while riding. The controls settings hold just the menu key.

# Aircraft
## The autopilot won't engage
The INS is still aligning (10 s standing, ATT / HDG flags), the power is off or the aircraft is on the ground.
## A dark cockpit
The battery is off, flat or removed - switch it on (the liner: BAT 1 and 2 on the overhead).
## The plane won't lift off
Too short a run: use a runway, flaps and the lift-off speed. On runway blocks the run is half as long.

# The liner
## TOGA LK and the thrust won't come back
The alpha floor fired. Press the A/THR disconnect (- by default).
## "AP OFF" keeps flashing
Press , (the red button) or MASTER WARN. "A/THR OFF" - the - button or MASTER CAUT.
## No autoland
The ILS station is not CAT III: it needs the flight inspection, a working monitor and clear critical areas. Are both APs and APPR on?
## An engine won't start
No bleed pressure (APU or X BLEED), ENG MODE not START, pumps off or no hydrogen. A hot start - ENG MASTER off and try again.

# Air defence and airport
## The air defence won't fire
By default the server only warns: fire is airdefense.mode = fire. It needs radars (for missiles a tracking one or a target within 48 blocks of the launcher), ammunition, power, a rule of engagement on the launcher itself (on "the post's marks" - an assignment or "Auto" on the KP) and the site's chunks loaded (a player near). With vehicles.wear = false shells hit but do no damage. In the usual mode never at your own, at 7700, the day code, own codes or the white list; a dark board's transponder does not answer.
## The air defence fires at my vehicle
A KP in the CODE-ONLY mode: only the key's code makes an aircraft a friend, not whose vehicle it is. Take a code card at the KP (right-click with a stack) and switch "Day key" on at the transponder's panel - the NO ID lamp goes out at the next interrogation. The card's term over or the owner pressed "Change the key" - a new card is needed. 7700 gives 180 s without fire, the KP's operator may give "Friendly for 10 min".
## The ILS doesn't work
Run the tablet's "ILS check" (Shift + right-click in the air): no shelter or power, a broken antenna element, a frequency conflict, the tower switched it off.
## The approach chart reads OUTDATED
The airport changed (code, runways, ILS) - print a new one at the tower.

# The book
## How do I get the book back?
Craft a new one: a book + metallurgical silicon. It is given only once.
## The checklist doesn't tick
It is live only in a pilot seat of the liner (book.liveChecklists). Elsewhere it is a plain list.
## Do recipes open NEI?
Yes, with NEI installed: left click - the recipe, right click - where used. Close NEI and the book comes back.
