# How it works
Radars see what flies, the command post (KP) keeps the tracks and decides by its rules of engagement, the AA gun and the missile system shoot. By default the server **only warns**: fire takes airdefense.mode = fire; the launchers linked to a CODE-ONLY KP fire without it too (airdefense.codeOnlyFire).
@note In the usual mode the air defence never fires at your own vehicles, code 7700, the code of the day, your own codes or the white list - a launcher without its KP neither: it interrogates the transponders itself; the access list's vehicles neither, unless the server turns airdefense.fireOnFriends on. In the CODE-ONLY mode nobody's vehicles are spared: without the key's code the air defence fires at yours too, 7700 has a 180 s grace (page "CODE-ONLY mode"). A transponder without the board's power or on a wreck does not answer: such an aircraft is silent to the air defence, its 7700 unheard.

# Transponder
On every vehicle, the ground ones too (airdefense.allTransponders): OFF / STBY / A (code 0000-7777) / C (+ altitude) / S (+ the board number). Its panel - the vehicle menu, "Transponder" line: digits, mode, 7000 / 7600 / 7700, "Day key", the DAY KEY and NO ID lamps. IDENT - the \ key: the blip blinks 18 s on every radar. A new vehicle - mode C, code 7000. Radars see no ground vehicle and nothing lower than 4 blocks over the ground: such a transponder is asked only while the vehicle is seen.
**Day key**: the owner's secret (one for all his KPs), the server's pepper and the key's generation give a code for each period - 20 min of the world's total time (airdefense.keyClock = total: /time set and sleeping do not change the code). For the first 60 s of a period the last one's code counts too.
**Code Card** (2 paper + blue dye): right-click a KP with a stack - the whole stack is printed (the owner and the access list). The card carries whose key, the generation and the term (1, 3 or 7 periods), never the code: the server works it out. A transponder with "Day key" sets the card's code from the pilot's inventory by itself (in the liner either pilot's, never a passenger's) and moves to the next period's code by itself; the card taken away - the card's code goes back to 7000. A card past its term or of an older key gives no code.

@kv Height finder (MV) | 400 blocks, the altitude of every target
**RCS** (detectability): ship 40, liner 20, shuttle 12, tiltrotor 8, helicopter 6, plane 4, jet 3 - the surveillance radar sees a plane at 337 blocks. Rain -15 %, a thunderstorm -30 % (where the radar stands: no rain in a desert); terrain casts a shadow, slabs, stairs and fences too, and in unloaded chunks by the heights the air defence remembered round its radars and launchers (airdefense.terrainCache); below 4 blocks over the ground and ground vehicles are unseen; the Singular Ship's invisibility hides it completely. Sounds: a ping for a new search radar, a steady tone while locked, a warble on a launch. Without power the RWR is silent; like every tuning it works only while the server has tuning on (vehicles.tuning).

# Zones and rules of engagement
**Zones** - forbidden, restricted, aerodrome, corridor; a circle or a (turned) rectangle, heights from-to. Pilots see them on the map, the HSI, the liner's ND and as a frame on the HUD; the prediction "ZONE ... ENTRY IN N S" (airdefense.zoneWarnSeconds = 30), within a third of it red with a second tone.
The **rules of engagement** are set on each launcher (its screen): hold fire / at zone violators / at all strangers / at the post's marks. The KP sets no rules: it assigns targets (a launcher on "the post's marks" takes them), switches "Auto" on (such a launcher takes violators itself) and "Track only" (a hold-fire for all of this KP's launchers). A launcher without a working KP fires only at violators. Before fire - a message to the pilot, a lock in 5 s, a wait (at least airdefense.minWarningSeconds), warning tracers, then fire.

@kv Missile system (EV) | 280 blocks, 4 tubes, two missiles in the air, at a tracking radar's target or its own eyes' (48 blocks), 110 a hit, 20,000 EU a launch
The gun leads a radar mark by its age, within 48 blocks takes the target with its own eyes and fires only with a clear line of fire; the first block stops a shell. A missile flies 3 blocks a tick for up to 8 s, a 3-block fuse, and loses its target behind terrain. Shells and missiles go into the launcher's screen, by Shift+right-click with them on it, or the "Puller" module takes them from chests nearby. The damage goes to the hull (armour -40 %, airdefense.damageMultiplier); with vehicles.wear = false hulls never wear, and the air defence fires to no effect.

# Command post
A map with zones, radars, launchers and tracks (own / stranger / violator / 7700), a data block, a contact list, a log, ALARM (redstone 15), "Auto", code card printing, a message to a pilot. Right-click a track: assign a launcher, auto, track only (this KP's launchers hold fire), "friend for 10 minutes", message. A track no surveillance radar has interrogated yet is "unknown": no fire. Radars and launchers answer to one KP of theirs (the ">" button on their screen).
- **"Zones"** tab: drag the corners, sides and centre with the mouse, the ring - turn, "Grid 8", copy / paste / undo.
- **"Settings"**: name, day key and secret, own codes, a Mode S white list ("23:Name" - board numbers repeat), trusted towers ("ICAO:Owner"), access.
- **"Equipment"**: energy, input, module slots.
- **"Identification"**: USUAL / CODE-ONLY, the cards' term, the options, "Change the key", the readiness line (the "CODE-ONLY mode" pages).
- The radar **coverage map** ("Cov.: 20 / 60 / 120 bl"): green - seen, red - dead zones behind terrain.

# CODE-ONLY mode
@note Without a code card a CODE-ONLY air defence fires at your vehicles too: only the key's code makes an aircraft a friend, not whose vehicle it is.
The KP's **"Identification"** tab: USUAL / CODE-ONLY. In CODE-ONLY a friend is only an aircraft whose transponder (A, C or S, the board powered) answers with the code of this air defence owner's key: set by a code card in the pilot's inventory or dialled by hand (the option "Code dialled by hand", on by default). The owner's and the access list's vehicles, the Mode S white list, trusted towers and the codes 7000 / 0000 / 7500 make nobody a friend, airdefense.fireOnFriends does not apply.
No match or no reply (STBY, OFF, a dark board) - a target, but only after two misses in a row at least 5 s apart (the "CHECK 1/2" tag). Then the launcher's stages: a message, a lock, a wait of at least 20 s for everybody, tracers, fire. Before each burst and launch the launcher silently looks at the transponder again: a match - the target is let go ("code accepted before fire").
**Fire with mode = warnings too.** A launcher linked to a CODE-ONLY KP fires at a board without the right code after the warnings even when the server is in the "warnings only" mode (airdefense.codeOnlyFire, on; false - as mode). Only once the mode is armed: while the switch-on countdown runs it does not fire. Everything else stays: PvP (airdefense.requirePvp), somebody aboard, private fields, the distance from the spawn, the wait of at least 20 s, the code check before the shot, 7700's grace, GUESSING. A usual KP's launchers and unlinked ones only warn with warnings.

# CODE-ONLY: switching on and the key
Only the owner switches it on (a server operator too - the KP's log says so, the owner is told), in two steps: a confirmation window with a summary ("Launchers: 2 - violators, 1 - all; airborne without the code: 3, yours: 1") and the button "All launchers: all without code", then arming in 60 s - a countdown on the KP's map, a chat line to the owner and the access list. While it counts down the identification is the usual one; switching off is immediate. The KP's day key switched off (or the server forbidding the mode until a restart) - once it is back, the mode arms anew, with the countdown and the chat line. Never without the day key (the KP's or airdefense.dayKey) nor with airdefense.codeOnly = false.
- **Card term**: 1, 3 or 7 periods (20 min, 1 h, 2 h 20 min).
- **"Change the key"** (two presses): the generation +1, every card given out is void, the access list is told to print new ones.
- **Options**: "Own codes count", "Code dialled by hand" (on), "Airport codes are friends" (a public aerodrome is no trap for guests), "Launchers to the access list" - without it only the owner changes the launchers' rule and link.
- **7700**: a 180 s grace per aircraft in all over a day, shared by all the owner's KPs (7700 - 7000 - 7700 does not reset it, the launchers' warning countdown only stops on 7700); the KP decides "Friendly / Check / Hostile", then a miss. **7600** - as in the usual mode, **7500** - a target and the KP's alarm.
- **Guessing**: with codes by hand or own codes, 4 different wrong codes within 2 min (each stood 3 s) from one aircraft or pilot - the GUESSING block for 5 min on all the owner's KPs. The operator lifts it: "Friendly for 10 min" or "Lift GUESSING".
- **Tracks**: CODE OK, NO CARD, NO REPLY, CHECK 1/2, 7700 1:20, GUESSING; a grey OWN / FRD - the owner's and his list's machines (information only, no effect on fire).

# CODE-ONLY: for the pilot
The owner or the access list of a code-only KP boarding without a good card is told in the chat "No code card: post ... (CODE-ONLY) will take this aircraft for a stranger". At the first interrogation without a match the amber flashing NO ID lamp lights - on the transponder's panel and the RWR panel, "NOT IDENTIFIED" on the HUD; 5 min before the card runs out - "CODE CARD: 5 MIN" (then the minutes left), in the chat at 5 and 1 min. A code-only KP's zones are signed "CODE-ONLY". The air defence never names the code itself. The KP's and the launcher's screens show "Fire by code: READY" or why not.
**Forgot the card.** Take a card at the KP (right-click with a stack) and switch "Day key" on - the aircraft is a friend at the next interrogation, and the launcher checks it once more before the shot; or dial the key's code by hand if the KP allows it: a dialled code counts once it stood 3 s, and the panel sends a new code at most once a second. With two owners' cards the code comes from the card of the owner whose CODE-ONLY KP is near (within 664 blocks), then of the nearest such KP's owner, then of a KP that lists you; of equal cards - the first in the inventory (the hotbar first): put the card you need first. Whose card works, the transponder panel shows ("Key: ..."). Old cards (with the code on the card itself) no longer work - print new ones. Or leave the area, land (radars see nothing below 4 blocks), squawk 7700 (180 s without fire) or ask the KP's operator for "Friendly for 10 min".
**A broken KP.** A broken KP deletes its data and zones: its launchers lose the link and work the usual way - in doubt, less fire. An unpowered or unloaded KP keeps the mode: the launcher takes it from the KP's data. Without a working KP the rule "all" comes down to "violators", as in the usual mode.

# A whole air-defence site
An example layout: the KP in the middle, the three radars behind it, the gun and the missile system in front. Each structure is placed as one block (cyan) and takes its other cells by itself - they must be free and not under someone else's protection. "Hologram in the world" shows it all at once.
@note The air defence works only in loaded chunks: a site with no player near (and no other mod's chunk loader) is loaded only by the intruder himself, within his view distance (about 160 blocks), not at the surveillance radar's 600 blocks. The air defence remembers the terrain of unloaded chunks, but radars, KPs and launchers in them stand still.

# Airport codes, IFF and 7600
- The tower gives codes 4100-4177: a departure with its take-off clearance, an arrival entering the zone with mode C. On its own only to the aircraft of its owner, his access list and its "Codes: trusted" list; to the others - the controller (his clearance or the "SQWK" button). It never replaces 7500 / 7600 / 7700 or a running day key, and a day key comes back on after leaving the airport. The liner gets it by CPDLC (WILCO sets it), the others have it set at once.
- An aircraft squawking the code its own or a trusted airport's tower gave this very aircraft, near the airport (160 blocks), is OWN: no alarm, no fire (airdefense.airportFriends). A zone violation and an IFF handoff weigh more than the code.
- **IFF**: the tower owner's radars interrogate aircraft. Silent or a stranger's code - the "INTRUD." alarm, a call to the pilot; he answers by switching the transponder on or replying by CPDLC. No answer within 30 s - handed to the air defence (airdefense.iffHandoff, off by default).
- **7600** (no radio): the KP shows a "NO RADIO" track with a 120 s timer and the decision "Friendly" (for 10 minutes), "Check" or "Hostile"; without a decision - the usual rules. A code change does not reset the timer (airdefense.noRadioMemoryMinutes) but drops "Check"; the time nobody sees the aircraft does not count. A launcher without a KP gives 7600 two minutes. 7500 - the KP's alarm.

# Modules and server rules
4 slots for core modules on each block: universal transformer, transformer, energy storage, economizer - everywhere; overclocker - radars and launchers (a KP has nothing to speed up), overdrive - radars only, puller - launchers only. A broken structure keeps its modules in its item.
Server rules (the airdefense category): enabled (off - the whole air defence stands unpowered), mode (warnings / fire), fireInProtectedRegions, fireOnFriends, requirePvp, fireOnUnmanned, maxLaunchersPerPlayer, maxRadarsPerPlayer, minDistanceFromSpawn, minWarningSeconds, launchLog (logs/airdefense.log: launches, who assigned the target, hits), damageMultiplier, terrainCache and terrainCacheRadius (the terrain memory, 640 bl), dayKey, keyClock (the day key's clock: total - the world's total time), zoneMaxDistance (a zone within 1024 blocks of its command post), maxZonesPerPlayer (64 zones a player), allTransponders (a transponder on every vehicle). The CODE-ONLY mode: codeOnly (false - forbidden, such KPs work the usual way), codeOnlyFire (fire with mode = warnings too, on), codeOnlyManual, codeOnlyArmSeconds, codeOnlyConfirmSeconds, codeOnlyMinWarningSeconds, codeOnlyEmergencySeconds (0 - 7700 is never a target), codeOnlyOverlapSeconds, codeCardMaxPeriods, codeOnlyProbeCodes, codeOnlyProbeMinutes. Command post orders - at most once a second.

# The embassy's air defence
An embassy with an airfield has its own air defence: 5 "Tuners' AA posts" (the core builds them, the add-on fires - shells, as its AA gun). The zone is 256 blocks from the embassy's core at any height; over a player's base (an active Field Generator's zone) there is none.
- **A target** is only a machine in the air with nobody admitted aboard. Admission is "Acquaintance" standing or higher at that embassy (and not its "Enemy"); a "Stranger", a "Violator" and an "Enemy" are not admitted. "Acquaintance" is earned by coming on the ground - on foot or by a Ring flight.
- **No target**: a player on foot, a player flying in armour or a jetpack, anything on the ground; a machine with any one admitted aboard - the pilot, the crew or a passenger; a player not admitted flying with an admitted squad mate within 64 blocks who is in the air too (a mate standing on the ground covers nobody).
- **The warning**: entering the zone - the embassy's red chat line, the same on the radio (CPDLC), "EMBASSY AIR DEFENCE: LEAVE THE ZONE" on the HUD and 10 s to turn away. Closer to the core by 32 blocks or below 40 blocks over the field within those seconds - the fire at once: landing during the warning is no way in. One warning a game day a player at each embassy: back into the zone the same day - the fire without a warning.
- **The fire** comes from the nearest post with a clear line, in bursts as the add-on's AA gun (110 blocks of range); the RWR sees the post. Out of the zone - "out of the zone", the engagement is over.
- **7700**: once a game day at an embassy - a 60 s pass without fire; a second time the same day - "7700 already used today", the fire is not held. Players' air defence still takes 7700 as an EMERGENCY.
- An **unmanned** machine in the air is a target after a warning to its owner if he is not admitted (offline counts as not admitted).
- Transponder codes and the players' CODE-ONLY mode play no part for the embassy: only the "Trust" decides.
- **The neutral sky**: players' air defence does not fire over the embassy's land, its field and its approach corridors (it only warns), and no forbidden zone of a command post can be set over it; a player's base in a corridor keeps its own defence.
Server rules (the airdefense category): embassyAd (off - the posts are decor), embassyWarnPerDay (1), embassyWarnSeconds (10), embassyCloseBlocks (32), embassyMinHeight (40), embassyUnmanned (on); all with enabled, regardless of mode and requirePvp.
