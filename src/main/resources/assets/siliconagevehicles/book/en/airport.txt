# Scale and layout
Airport scale: **1 block = 10 m-eq**. The angles are real: the outer marker is 720 blocks out (7.2 km), the threshold crossing height 1.5 blocks (15 m). Aircraft are not rescaled.
Below is the full ILS runway layout (as the "Airport Planner Tablet" shows it): the runway, its ends, the localizer beyond the far end, the glide slope mast at the near one, the shelters, markers, RVR, PAPI, the weather station and windsock, the approach lights; critical areas amber, sensitive ones faint. "Hologram in the world" lays it out from the block you look at (the landing threshold) in the direction you look.
@schem airport

# A runway by its ends
Two **"Runway End"** blocks facing each other make a runway: the markings (piano keys, number, the parallel's letter, centre line, touchdown zone, aiming point, side stripes; width 3-9) and the lights appear by themselves.
- Lights: edge (yellow over the last 600 m-eq), centre line, touchdown zone, green threshold, red end, a 900 m-eq ALS with the sequenced flasher.
- 5 brightness steps; AUTO - at night, in rain and with RVR under 1500. The "TOWER" mode - the lights follow the tower.
- Runway numbers from the magnetic course (airport.magneticVariation), parallels L / C / R.
@recipe wb:runwayEnd

# ILS: localizer, glide slope, shelter
- **Localizer** (a 5 x 1 array) - beyond the far end on the centre line, the elements across it. Place it looking along the runway toward the landing threshold.
- **Glide slope mast** (3 blocks) - at the near end, 12-18 blocks off the centre line, about 25 in.
- An **ILS Shelter** near each antenna - EU power (4 module slots) and the monitor.
- The antennas pair into the runway's station by themselves: ident and frequency 108.10-111.95 on the LOC (the G/S paired), the ident in Morse with LS pressed.
- Beams: LOC +-2.5 deg full scale, a back course; G/S +-0.7 deg, false glide slopes at 6 and 9 deg.

# Categories and critical areas
The station's category - **CAT I / II / III** - comes from the antennas' placement, the monitor, the critical and sensitive areas and the flight inspection:
- a block or a vehicle in a **critical** area - CAT I and a bent beam; in a **sensitive** one - CAT II;
- a new or changed station is CAT I until the **flight inspection**: the first approach by any aircraft with its transponder on calibrates it ("CALIBRATED, CAT III" or lower with a hint);
- the **monitor** shuts the station off on a broken antenna, a beam out of tolerance or no power - red LOC / G/S flags on the PFD and "ILS FAULT".
@note The liner's autoland needs CAT III. On CAT I / II the autopilot disconnects at the minimum.

# Markers, DME, RVR, weather
- **Markers** OM / MM / IM on the extended centre line (720 / 105 / 30 blocks): a tone and a PFD lamp.
- **DME** - the distance in km-eq on the PFD and ND.
- **RVR** - TDZ / MID / END transmissometers: the visual range (rain, thunder, fog, cloud, night) and the category needed on the ND and the MCDU.
- **Weather station** and **windsock**: the wind (gusting in thunderstorms), QNH, temperature - in the ATIS, on the ND, PFD and MCDU.

# Lights and PAPI
PAPI - 4 lights left of the runway abeam the touchdown point: two white and two red - on the glide slope. ALS - the approach lights with the sequenced flasher, switched on at the runway end. The runway, approach and taxiway lights are run from the tower (AUTO or 0-5).
@recipe wb:papi

# Airport Planner Tablet
- Right-click the two runway ends (the landing one first) - held, it shows the hologram: where the LOC, the mast, the shelters, markers, RVR, PAPI, weather, ALS and lights go; the critical areas red with something in them; the category expected.
- Shift + right-click a runway block - places both runway ends at its ends.
- Shift + right-click in the air - "ILS check": every issue of the station with a hint. Right-click in the air - reset.
@recipe wb:aptPlanner

# Control tower
The tower (HV, 6 blocks: a shaft and a glazed cab) makes an airport of the runways within 15 km-eq: an ICAO code (4 letters), a name, the runway in use, the lights, the ILS, traffic and weather. MV power 32 EU/t, 4 module slots, an owner and access. Unpowered it is offline.
Its screen: TRAFFIC (scope, strips, the controller's clearances), RUNWAYS (ILS, RVR, lights), ATIS, MAP (approaches, the taxi graph, printing charts), LOG, SETTINGS.

@recipe wb:tower

# ATIS, CPDLC and the automatic tower
- **ATIS**: the information letter moves on when something that matters changed (runway, approach, wind, visibility, QNH). In the MCDU (ATC COMM - ATIS) and in the chat of pilots within 30 km-eq.
- **CPDLC** (the MCDU's ATC key - "ATC COMM"): requests, WILCO / UNABLE / STANDBY, LOAD a value into the FCU, the "ATC MSG" buttons. Aircraft without an MCDU get it in the chat and as a HUD line.
- The **automatic tower** with no controller: one aircraft on the runway, landing priority, an interval (airport.autoInterval), go-arounds. A player in the seat replaces it.

# Approaches, charts, taxiing
- **Approaches**: for each ILS runway the tower publishes ILSxx: CIxx (10 km-eq), FFxx (5.5 km-eq, on the glide slope), the threshold, the missed approach MAxx and transitions from the entry fixes ICAO + N / E / S / W. In the MCDU - ARRIVAL, the ILS tunes itself.
- **Approach Chart** (item) - printed by the tower; the airport changed - an old chart reads OUTDATED.
- **Taxi lights** (4 kinds): green centre line, blue edges, a stop bar, a stand. A taxi clearance lights a follow-the-greens route (airport.taxiAhead lights ahead), the stop bar goes out for the take-off clearance.

# The embassy airfield
The Tuners' new embassies (with this add-on) have an airfield: a 200 x 9 runway along the main street beside the dome (FULL); on cramped land a 90 x 7 runway and an "H" helipad (SHORT) or the "H" alone (HELI). A terminal, a control tower with a beacon, an apron with stands 1...4, a hangar, service columns and the monument "The Lithographers' Last Shuttle". The add-on places no block of its own there: the field's airport is virtual, made from the core's data and never saved. Old embassies get no field.
- **The airport**: its ICAO code is X, two letters of the element and the number's letter ("Tantalum-1" - XTAA); player towers can't take an X code. Runway numbers follow the true course. The embassy tower is online while someone in the Overworld is within 3000 blocks; the automatic tower controls it.
- **Admission**: the embassy's sky lets in players of "Acquaintance" standing and higher (the air defence chapter, "The embassy's air defence"); "Acquaintance" is earned by coming on the ground. An "Enemy" gets "UNABLE: YOU ARE AN ENEMY OF THE EMBASSY" by CPDLC; 7700 is accepted from anyone.
- **ATIS** of an embassy tower reaches the chat only within 600 blocks or when the embassy is the MCDU's destination; in the MCDU (ATC COMM - ATIS) - always. The MCDU, the ND and the vehicle map show an embassy only if you have been there or it is within 1024 blocks (source "EMBASSY").
- **ILS** - on FULL only, CAT III at once ("the Tuners' flight check"): the localizer beyond the far end, a 3 deg glide slope (airport.embassyGlideAngle; at 3.5 - CAT II only). The low edge of the land right by the field (grass, dirt, sand, gravel, snow - up to two blocks over the runway's level) does not spoil the station; machines, buildings, hills, stone and ice in its areas lower the category as at your own stations. The thresholds are displaced by 20 blocks, the landing distance is 181: a liner lands with the autobrake LO or higher.
- **Transponder codes** of an embassy tower are 5100-5177, for everyone but an "Enemy". Players' air defence does not trust those codes.
- **The dome**: no landing over the dome - "NO LANDING INSIDE THE DOME" by CPDLC and a warning to the pilot. A SHORT field takes no liner (airport.embassyLinerShort); light machines land freely.
- **Lights**: the core lays the edge, threshold, end and taxi lights and the markings as blocks; the add-on adds the centre line, the touchdown zone, the approach lights with the sequenced flasher and the PAPI - 5 steps and AUTO, as on its own runways.

# Parking, services and the "Logbook"
- **Parking**: a machine stopped on a stand of its kind (the liner - stand 1, planes - stands 2...4, helicopters and tiltrotors - "H") is booked to its owner; at most 2 stands a player at one embassy. The first day is free, then 1 chip a day, free while you hold a valid talon of that embassy; owed for more than a day - the machine is towed to the "Overflow" lot. The days count for the whole stay: driving off a stand and back on, a tow or the Overflow lot give no new free day, and on the lot the debt grows the same way - it is paid with the tow back to a stand.
- **Towing**: a machine with nobody aboard on the runway, a pad or the taxiway, or on a stand it does not hold or fit (a buggy on stand 2, a plane on the "H"), for more than 2 min is towed to a free stand of its kind (none free - to the "Overflow" lot); on the apron off the stands for more than 10 min - the same; under the dome for more than 5 min - to the Overflow lot. A machine never disappears. A machine given away stays on its stand, now its new owner's.
- **The service column** (right-click; sneaking - the core's armour gases): your machines within 16 blocks of the column and on this embassy's stands. Charging the battery and the buffer - 1 chip per 2 M EU (at most 16 M at once and 32 M a day), hydrogen - 1 chip per 8 000 mB (at most 48 000 at once and 64 000 a day), repair in the hangar - 1 chip per 10% of wear (at most 60% at once), "Electrolyte" - 2 chips, towing your own machine (the day's first is free), an approach chart on your own paper (the first is free). Charging, hydrogen, repair and "Electrolyte" from "Acquaintance" on; parking and the approach chart from "Stranger", towing from "Violator"; a "Stranger" or a "Violator" gets an emergency refuel and charge up to 25% of the tank and the buffer once a day. The embassy prices it by your standing and takes the chips.
- **The "Logbook"**: the first landing at each embassy (flown in from beyond the field, the transponder on, the machine stopped on a stand or the "H") - 2 chips and +5 "Trust", once an embassy. A machine set down on the field from the inventory and hopped is no landing.
- A ship's **jump** onto an embassy's airfield lands only on the "H"; onto the runway it is refused; into the embassy's air defence zone (256 blocks from the core) only with someone admitted aboard or his 7700 pass running.
- The embassy Dispatcher's "Airfield" tab: your machines on the stands, the day's prices, the tower, the ATIS and the "Service" button.
Server rules (the airport category): embassyAirports, embassyServices, embassyTowing, embassyAtisRange (600), embassyTowMinutes (2), embassyDomeMinutes (5), embassyApronMinutes (10), embassyGlideAngle (3.0), embassyLinerShort (off).
